# Akeel Ather Medina

I'm interested in the systems side of machine learning: inference, memory hierarchies, and interconnects. I've recently finished an MSc in High Performance Computing at the University of Edinburgh, where my thesis profiles the cost (energy, time) of KV-cache offloading during inference, measured on H200 (PCIe) and GH200 (NVLink-C2C) systems. I was a part of TeamEPCC in the ISC 26 High Performance Student Cluster Competition, working on optimizing `llama.cpp` inference and aggregate multi-client parallel file IO throughput across a hetergenous cluster with BeeGFS. I have 2 years of work experience at an Australian startup, Abyss Solutions, working on point-cloud analysis at scale. I’m currently applying for relevant PhD positions!
