# Akeel Ather Medina

I'm broadly interested in the systems side of machine learning and HPC. See more [here](about).

I recently finished an MSc in High Performance Computing at the University of Edinburgh, where my thesis profiled the time and energy cost of KV-cache offloading during LLM inference on H200 (PCIe) and GH200 (NVLink-C2C) systems. With TeamEPCC at the ISC26 Student Cluster Competition, I optimized `llama.cpp` inference under a power cap and built a BeeGFS parallel file system across a heterogeneous cluster. Before that, I spent two years at Abyss Solutions, an Australian startup, building point-cloud analysis pipelines at scale.

I'm currently looking for PhD positions and engineering roles in GPU systems and LLM inference.
